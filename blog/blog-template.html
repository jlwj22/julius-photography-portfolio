A few new experiences, being myself actually pays off.

June 2, 2025

#Photography #Experiences #Realization

As I continue to explore the world through my lens, I've had some new experiences that have truly shaped my perspective. It's fascinating how being myself and embracing my unique style has led to unexpected opportunities and connections.

Recently, I had the chance to actually get outside to see more things and go experience new places. I haven't even been able to update my blog because of how busy I am. Now, those who throw events within the city are reaching out to me to take photos of their events. It's a surreal feeling, knowing that my work is being recognized and appreciated by others.

Although I am new, I am learning incredibly fast, almost as if I'm a sponge to the information given to me. I was able to learn about how I should price my work and the importance of building relationships with clients.

Here are some key takeaways from my recent experiences:

  • Embrace your unique style and perspective.
  • Networking and building relationships can open doors to new opportunities.
  • Always be open to learning and adapting to new challenges.
  • Photography is not just about capturing images; it's about telling stories and connecting with people.
  • Investing in your craft and continuously improving is essential for growth.
  • Don't be afraid to put yourself out there and showcase your work.
  • Every experience, whether good or bad, contributes to your journey as a photographer.

I can't explain how much fun I've been having recently. I bring my camera everywhere I go now. I make sure that I'm not missing any moment or any opportunity that I can get my hands on. I even got my own booking recently—how crazy can that be? I didn't even think that I would be able to get a booking this early in my career, but here I am, and I am loving every moment of it.

I can't wait to see where this journey takes me next. I'll try my best to continue to create and update blog posts about my experiences, but work is working, and taking photos is taking photos. But I will try my best to keep you all updated on my journey as a photographer. Thank you for following along.

Julius L. Jones Jr.

Student, photographer, and storyteller capturing moments that matter.
